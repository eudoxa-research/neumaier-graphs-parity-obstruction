# A parity obstruction for Neumaier graphs with e = s − 2

**Bryan Zachary Bu, Karam Gill, Hyunjun Yi**

September 2026. Accepted for JMM 2027 (AMS Contributed Paper Session on Graph Theory).

DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.23343392](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23343392)

## Abstract

We prove that if μ is odd then 4 divides s. This explains why these parameter sets vanish in the computations of Abiad, De Boeck and Zeijlemaker and answers their question. We also eliminate five further open parameter sets from their table.
